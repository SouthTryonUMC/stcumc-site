Trinity's Table

A hot meal, twice a week, in the fellowship hall at 2516 South Tryon Street. It costs nothing and there is nothing to sign.

How it works

Doors open and people come in. Somebody usually prays. Plates go out at the serving window, and folks find a seat wherever there's room.

Some people eat and go. Some stay the whole time and talk. Both are fine.

You are not a guest of this church when you sit down. You're a neighbor, and this is your table too.

House rules

What we ask of each other.

We want everybody who walks in to get fed, and the food is not unlimited. So there are a few things we ask.

  • One plate per person. If you need more than that, come see the kitchen staff and we will work it out with you.
  • Two drinks per meal, and please don't fill water bottles from the dispenser.
  • Look out for each other. This room stays safe because the people in it decide to keep it that way.

None of this is a condition of being welcome. You are welcome either way.

Help out

Serve with us.

The kitchen can always use hands, on a Tuesday, a Thursday, or once a month. You don't need experience and you don't need to be a member here.

We also take donations of food and supplies. Get in touch before you bring anything so we know what's coming and have somewhere to put it.
