If this would be your first time.

Walking into a church you've never been to is its own kind of hard. Here is most of what you'd probably want to know before you decide.

When is worship?

Sunday at 11:00 AM. It runs about an hour and fifteen minutes.

Where are you?

2516 South Tryon Street, Charlotte, NC 28203, between Brookhill Village and Southside Homes.

What should I wear?

Whatever you have on. Some people dress up because they like to. Nobody is checking.

What happens at Communion?

We celebrate Communion on the first Sunday of each month. The table is open to anyone who wants to come to it. You don't have to be a member here or a member anywhere.

Do I have to sign anything or give money?

No. There's an offering, and you're free to let it pass. Nobody will follow up with you unless you ask us to.

What if I'm coming for the meal instead?

Trinity's Table is Tuesday and Thursday at 11:30 AM, same building. There's nothing else you need to know ahead of time. Just come. You can read more about the table here.
