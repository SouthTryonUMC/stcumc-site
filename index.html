Our vision

A neighborhood church transforming lives with love, justice, and community.

Trinity's Table

A hot meal, twice a week.

We set the tables in the fellowship hall on Tuesday and Thursday and serve lunch at 11:30. Anyone can come. You don't have to sign anything, prove anything, or belong to this church.

Most weeks somebody prays before the plates go out, and most weeks people stay a while after they've eaten.

More about the table
Sunday Worship

Eleven o'clock, about an hour and fifteen minutes.

Communion is the first Sunday of the month and the table is open. You don't have to be a member and you don't have to have your life in order.

People show up in work clothes, in Sunday clothes, and in whatever they had on. All of that is fine.

What to expect
Our neighborhood

Brookhill Village and Southside Homes.

These two neighborhoods have held families for generations. Rents are climbing, redevelopment is closing in, and a lot of people who built their lives here are being pushed toward the door.

We are not neutral about that. We also keep the building open while the arguing goes on.
