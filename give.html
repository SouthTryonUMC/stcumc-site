What your giving supports

Gifts to South Tryon Community UMC pay for the meals at Trinity's Table, the utilities that keep this building running, and the daily work of a church that stays open to its neighbors. Every gift is used here, in this neighborhood.

Ways to give

Online. One-time or recurring gifts through Tithe.ly. It takes about a minute.

By mail. South Tryon Community Mission (Charlotte) UMC, 2516 South Tryon Street, Charlotte, NC 28203.

In person. In the plate on Sunday, or hand it to someone in the office during the week.
